# AI IDEA — CabinetMakerPro

AI IDEA is a workshop-focused assistant integration for SketchUp 2025 and interior manufacturing workflows.

## Status
Development scaffold. No production CNC output. All geometry must be verified inside SketchUp and against the approved workshop drawings.

## Confirmed workshop defaults
- Units: millimeters
- Common cabinet MDF: 17 mm
- Common back panel: 7 mm
- Kitchen cabinet plinth: 100 mm
- Other cabinets: 50 mm or 17 mm, only as specified by their approved design

## Development
Active development happens on feature branches. `main` remains a stable index until components have been validated.

## Safety
Generated geometry, parts lists and CNC programs require real equipment and manufacturing checks before production.
